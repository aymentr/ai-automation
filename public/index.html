Questions, answered

When does Autonoma launch?

We're rolling out early access this fall. Waitlist members get first access, in order of signup.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Describe what you want in plain English, or use the visual builder. Developers can drop into code for advanced logic if they want to.

What tools does Autonoma work with?

We're launching with native connectors for the most common CRMs, inboxes, docs, and spreadsheets, plus a general API/webhook connector for everything else.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Secrets are encrypted at rest, every agent action is scoped and logged, and sensitive steps can require human approval before they run.

How much will it cost?

Pricing will be announced closer to launch. Waitlist members will get an early-access discount.
